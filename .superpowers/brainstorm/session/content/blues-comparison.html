Why Balanced Blue?

For an energy-tracking productivity app, the balanced approach works best:

  • Soft on eyes — Your users will stare at this for hours. Desaturated blues reduce fatigue.
  • Energy level compatibility — Blues naturally feel calm, matching low-energy states, while the lighter shades feel fresh and energizing for high-focus periods.
  • Analytics readability — Charts and graphs benefit from the subtle contrast between shades.
  • Distinctive enough — Not generic like pure blue, but professional enough for a productivity tool.
